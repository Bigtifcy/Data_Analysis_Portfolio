# 👨‍💼 Employee Overview

## 📌 Overview

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of an Employee Overview Dataset, providing valuable insights into workforce demographics, salary distribution, departmental performance, employee status, and organizational trends. The dashboard was developed to help organizations understand their workforce better and support data-driven human resource decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The primary objective of this analysis is to:

- Analyze employee demographics and workforce composition.
- Evaluate salary distribution across departments and job roles.
- Identify departmental workforce strength.
- Understand employee performance and organizational trends.
- Discover patterns that can improve workforce planning and decision-making.
- Develop an interactive dashboard for HR and business stakeholders.

---

## 📊 Dataset

**Source:** Kaggle


The dataset contains employee-related information such as demographic details, department, job role, salary, age, gender, education, experience, and other HR-related attributes.

The data was cleaned, transformed, and prepared using Microsoft Excel and Power BI before visualization.

## 🔍 Insights

### 👥 Employee Demographics

- The workforce consists of employees across different age groups and genders.
- The majority of employees fall within the organization's active working-age population.
- Gender representation varies across departments.

### 🏢 Department Analysis

- Some departments employ significantly more staff than others.
- Workforce allocation highlights the organization's operational priorities.
- Departments with lower employee counts may require additional workforce planning.

### 💰 Salary Analysis

- Salary varies across departments and job roles.
- Certain positions contribute to higher payroll costs due to specialized responsibilities.
- Salary distribution provides insight into compensation structure and budgeting.

### 📊 Workforce Distribution

- Employee distribution across departments reveals organizational structure.
- Job role analysis identifies the most common positions within the company.
- Workforce composition can support recruitment and succession planning.

### 📈 Business Insights

- Data-driven workforce analysis helps identify opportunities for improving employee management.
- HR teams can leverage these insights for better recruitment, compensation planning, and resource allocation.
- Interactive dashboards enable stakeholders to monitor workforce trends efficiently.
